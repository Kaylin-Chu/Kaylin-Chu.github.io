What benefits did you experience from following your learning and wellbeing plan?

What does my stress profile look like? What does stress look like externally (how might others tell)? What does it feel like internally (how might you know)? What do you need when you notice you are stressed?

I like to think I’m quite aware of my emotions and know when I’m stressed, during bootcamp I’ve kind of foregone my responsibilities so the only task I have at the moment is studying, I try to finish assessments as they pop up and that keeps me relaxed. Getting a good sleep helps to keep me calm and my partner is pretty good at helping me drift off, he gets my water bottle, turns the light off and rubs my back till I fall asleep.Stress to me has physical signs - tight chest / heart beating faster / breathless and I can be grumpy or mopey - given up and quit the stress. I find visible progress helpful for de-stressing.
